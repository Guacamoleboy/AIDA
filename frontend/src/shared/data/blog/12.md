# Choosing and defining my project

After exploring several possible directions with Engestofte Gods, I have now chosen a project. The MVP focuses on wedding enquiries rather than the Christmas market.

The problem is that a wedding enquiry can contain many details and repeated clarifications. Customers may need help describing their wishes, while Johan needs one structured source of truth instead of information spread across messages and planning tools.

The solution starts at `engestofte-gods.dk/kontakt`. The customer sees the Engestofte Gods presentation, smiley-report, leadership images and language options. A button opens a separate AI-flow where the customer answers one relevant question at a time.

The AI-flow collects information about the date, guests, ceremony, food, extras, accommodation, transport and special requirements. It can identify missing or conflicting information and suggest relevant, non-binding extras. The unfinished request is stored locally until the customer logs in or creates an account.

After authentication, the request is shown under `/dashboard/events/`. Johan can review and approve it. Once approved, the customer receives access to `/dashboard/events/{id}`, which is the customer’s own event platform with event data and event-specific Messenger communication.

The project keeps Johan in control of availability, prices, changes and final approval. The AI supports the process, but it does not replace the human decision-maker.
